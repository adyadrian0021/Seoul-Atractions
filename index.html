Seoul 1

Seoul (officially Seoul Special Metropolitan City) is the capital and largest city of South Korea, sitting along the Han River in the northwestern part of the country. Spanning about 605 km² (234 sq mi) across 25 districts, the city itself has roughly 9.6 million residents, while the wider Seoul Metropolitan Area holds more than half of South Korea's population.
